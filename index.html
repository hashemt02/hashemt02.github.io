05 / ABOUT

About me

I’m drawn to understanding technology from the fundamentals up: the physics behind a device, the properties of its materials, and the choices that shape what it can do. I enjoy tracing a complex system back to those foundations and understanding both its capabilities and its limits.

That curiosity carries into my work across hardware and software, from radar electronics to the code that controls them and processes their data. Through my EECS studies at UC Berkeley, research at Berkeley Lab, and experience building Bytes, I’m learning to connect that understanding with practical engineering. I’m especially interested in embedded systems, wireless/RF, and digital IC design.
